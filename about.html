About

I'm a research scientist at Nuro, where I post-train flow-based driving policies with reinforcement learning so that autonomous vehicles drive safely and like a human would.

My research is about robots that notice they're going wrong and do something about it. In SAILOR (NeurIPS 2025 Spotlight) we learn a world model, a reward model and a critic from demonstrations alone, then search over imagined futures at test time to correct a base policy mid-rollout, beating diffusion policies trained on 5–10× more data.

Before Nuro I finished my MS at the CMU Robotics Institute, and before that a dual degree at IIT Kharagpur. Along the way I've built a fair number of robots: a stair-climbing terrace-farming robot, an autonomous lunar excavator, a self-fabricated ground vehicle that placed twice at IGVC, and a 1/10-scale racecar that learned to drive entirely inside a dream.
